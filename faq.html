Products

Our range covers interior and exterior walls, ceilings, roofs, metal and wood. Each product lists its recommended surfaces – see the Products page to filter by the surface you're painting.

We recommend two coats for an even, durable finish. A third coat may be needed when making a dramatic colour change (e.g. dark to light). New or porous surfaces should be primed first. Our Paint Calculator works out quantities for any number of coats.

Water-based emulsions are typically touch dry in about an hour and can be recoated after 2–4 hours. Solvent-based enamels take longer – usually overnight before recoating. Drying is slower in cool or humid conditions, so always check the tin for exact guidance.

Allow new paint around two weeks to fully cure. Then gently wipe marks with a soft cloth or sponge and mild soapy water, and rinse with clean water. Avoid abrasive cleaners or scourers, which can damage the finish.
